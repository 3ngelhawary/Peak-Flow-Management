Calculation Concept

Rational mode: peak flow is calculated from Q = C × i × A / 360, then distributed using a simplified triangular hydrograph (Tp = storm duration / 2). Select intensity i from your local IDF curve at the duration equal to the system's Time of Concentration and your target return period (e.g. 10-year, 100-year).

SCS mode: 24-hour Type II cumulative rainfall is scaled to the input storm depth, converted to CN runoff, then routed through an NRCS dimensionless unit hydrograph. The user inputs Tc (Time of Concentration); the model internally derives Tp (Time to Peak) as Tp = (Δt/2) + 0.6 × Tc. Tc max is 360 min for a 24-hour storm.

Direct Inflow: a constant flow (L/sec) added to the storm hydrograph throughout the entire simulation, including after the storm ends until the tank fully drains. Use this to represent a forcemain, pump station discharge, or any external pipe disposing into the network at a fixed rate regardless of rainfall.

Infiltration: loss rate Qinf = f × Ainf(h), where f is the design infiltration rate (mm/hr) and h is the current water depth. None models a sealed tank. Base only (lined walls, earth floor) uses the base area L × W whenever water is present. Base + walls (fully earthen basin) adds the wetted side area 2h√(1+z²)(L + W + 2zh), so losses grow as the basin fills. A constant rate is applied during and after the storm (no head dependence). Enter a design value, i.e. the tested rate divided by an appropriate factor of safety for clogging and uncertainty.

Evaporation: loss rate Qevap = e × As(h), with e the open-water evaporation (mm/day) and As = (L + 2zh)(W + 2zh) the water surface area. Optionally applied only after rainfall ends (storm duration for Rational, 24 h for SCS). Set e = 0 for covered or buried tanks.

Basin geometry: a rectangular base L × W with side slope z (H:1V) gives the stage–storage relation V(h) = LWh + (L + W)zh² + (4/3)z²h³. Water depth is solved from the stored volume each step, and the full depth is solved from Tank Capacity.

Max Factored Volume: maximum storage reached during simulation × safety factor. This is the required tank design size.

Emptying Time: time to drain the actual peak volume (unfactored) by the pump plus any infiltration and evaporation, net of any direct inflow still arriving, from the drawdown integral t = ∫ As(h) / [Qpump + Qinf(h) + Qevap(h) − Qdirect] dh between 0 and the peak depth. With no losses this reduces to peak volume ÷ (pump rate − direct inflow). "Won't drain" means the outflow cannot exceed the direct inflow. Use this to verify inter-storm recovery. With no pump, the animated drain-out stops 72 h after runoff ends, but Emptying Time is still calculated in full.

Volume balance: total inflow = pumped + infiltrated + evaporated + overflow + volume still stored. The closure error is reported below the results as a check on the routing.

Peak Inflow & Peak Time: maximum total inflow rate reached during the simulation and the clock time at which it occurred. Includes both storm runoff and direct inflow.

Overflow Volume: total volume lost when the tank exceeded capacity. A non-zero value means the tank is undersized or the pump is too slow.

Best storage duration: the critical duration is the one producing the highest max factored volume while emptying time remains acceptable.
